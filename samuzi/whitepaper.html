01 · Introduction

What is Samuzi NFT?

Samuzi NFT is a digital art-based collectible project inspired by the fusion of futuristic samurai culture, cyberpunk storytelling, and streetwear-inspired visual identity. Each Samuzi character represents individuality, creative rebellion, and participation in a community-powered Web3 movement.

The project is not only about collecting artwork. It is about building a long-term digital brand that can connect art, community, collaborations, digital access, creative content, and future utility layers.

00 · Akamai Land

The next Samuzi expansion

Akamai Land is a land-based NFT game developed by Samuzi NFTs. Every Akamai Land NFT represents a unique 10×10 digital land plot inside a 10,000 land collection. Land ownership creates a permanent digital presence inside the Akamai ecosystem and connects holders to future earning, participation, and utility mechanics.

10,000 Land NFTs

A large digital land collection built for ecosystem participation and long-term community growth.

10×10 Digital Plots

Each NFT represents a unique digital land plot inside the Akamai world.

Daily Streaks

Players can improve land performance by maintaining daily earning streaks through land-based tasks.

Redeemable Digital Items

50 special traits in the new phase will grant access to claim digital items inside narrrfs.world.

00A · Akamai Economy

Mint and royalty distribution

Akamai Land is designed to support project development, daily participation, raffle rewards, and Samuzi Token strength through a clear distribution model.

Mint fund distribution

  • 70% → Project development and ecosystem growth.
  • 20% → Daily raffle pool for land owners.
  • 10% → Samuzi Token buyback support.

Royalty distribution

  • 3% → Project development.
  • 1% → Daily raffle pool.
  • 1% → Samuzi Token buyback pool.

Rewards, utility, gameplay features, dates, and mechanics may change as the ecosystem develops. Official details should always be verified through Samuzi channels before taking action.
