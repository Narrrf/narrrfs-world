## 🎯 **WHAT IS NARRRFS WORLD?**

Narrrfs World is a comprehensive gaming ecosystem featuring **7 interactive games**, a **Discord community bot**, an **NFT verification system**, and a **DSPOINC token economy**. Players earn DSPOINC (the in-game currency) by playing games, completing quests, and participating in community events.

### **The Big Picture:**
- **7 Games:** Tetris, Snake, Space Invaders, Cheese Hunt, Discord Race, Cheese Rumble, and a 3D Riddle Game
- **Discord Bot:** 50+ commands for community management and gameplay
- **Admin Interface (Nerd Lab):** 17 tabs for complete system management
- **NFT Integration:** Holder verification with automatic Discord role granting
- **Staking System:** Players can freeze DSPOINC tokens for rewards
- **Store System:** In-game purchases with inventory management
- **Achievement System:** 73+ achievements across multiple games

## 💰 **DSPOINC STAKING SYSTEM**

**What it is:** Players can "freeze" their DSPOINC tokens for a set period to earn rewards.

**How it works:**
1. Player chooses amount to stake (freeze)
2. Player chooses duration (1, 3, 6, 12, 24, or 36 months)
3. DSPOINC is frozen and cannot be spent
4. When the freeze period ends, player gets original amount + reward
5. Reward rate increases with longer freeze periods (5% to 50%)

**Early Unstake Option:**
- Players can unstake early with a 15% penalty
- 85% of frozen amount is returned
- Penalty is permanent (not refundable)

**Where to access:** `stake-lab.html` page on the website.

## 🎴 **NFT VERIFICATION SYSTEM**

**What it is:** System that verifies NFT ownership and grants Discord roles automatically.

**How it works:**
1. Player connects their wallet on the website
2. System checks if wallet owns NFTs from approved collections
3. If verified, player automatically gets Discord roles (VIP, Genesis, etc.)
4. Roles unlock special benefits and multipliers

**Collections Supported:**
- VIP Collection
- Genesis Collection
- Other approved NFT collections

**Where to access:** Holder verification page on the website.

## 🏪 **STORE SYSTEM**

**What it is:** In-game store where players buy items with DSPOINC.

**How it works:**
1. Players browse items in the store
2. Items have prices in DSPOINC
3. Players purchase items (DSPOINC is deducted)
4. Items are added to player inventory
5. Items can be used or displayed in profile

**Item Types:**

- Special items
- Limited edition items

**Where to access:** Store section on `profile.html` or via Discord bot `/store` command.

## 📊 **SEASON SYSTEM**

**What it is:** Game data is organized into "seasons" - periods of time where scores and achievements are tracked separately.

**How it works:**
- Each season has a name (e.g., "Season 5 - The Ultimate Cheese Challenge")
- Player scores are tracked per season
- Leaderboards are season-specific
- At season end, data is preserved but new season starts fresh
- Admin can create unlimited seasons

**Why it matters:** Keeps the game fresh and gives new players a chance to compete.

## 🎯 **QUEST SYSTEM**

**What it is:** Missions and challenges that players can complete for DSPOINC rewards.

**How it works:**
1. Admin creates quests with requirements
2. Players complete quests (e.g., "Play 10 games of Tetris")
3. Players submit claims for quest completion
4. Admin approves or rejects claims
5. Approved claims grant DSPOINC rewards

**Quest Types:**
- Game-based quests (play X games)
- Achievement quests (unlock X achievements)
- Social quests (Twitter missions)
- Special event quests

## 📞 **SUPPORT & TROUBLESHOOTING**

### **Common Player Issues:**

**"I can't log in"**
- Check if Discord OAuth is working
- Verify player has Discord account
- Check browser cookies/session

**"My balance is wrong"**
- Check Recent Score Changes in profile
- Verify no active stakes (frozen balance)
- Check admin interface for recent adjustments

**"I can't see my achievements"**
- Verify player has completed requirements
- Check achievement system is active
- Review achievement gallery on profile

**"Store purchase failed"**
- Check available balance (excludes frozen DSPOINC)
- Verify item is in stock
- Check store system status
