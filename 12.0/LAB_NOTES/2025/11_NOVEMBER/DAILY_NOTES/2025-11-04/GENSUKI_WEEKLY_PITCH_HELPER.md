## 🎤 **PRESENTATION TIPS**

### **Energy & Tone:**
- Start energetic (hook them in first 30 seconds)
- Use pauses for emphasis
- Vary your pace (slow for important points, fast for excitement)
- Smile when talking (they can hear it!)

### **Technical Balance:**
- Don't overwhelm with code details
- Focus on user benefits, not implementation
- Use analogies ("like a gaming dashboard")
- Keep jargon to minimum

### **Engagement:**
- Ask rhetorical questions ("Have you seen your rank yet?")
- Use "we" language (inclusive)
- Reference community members if possible
- Encourage questions at the end

### **Visual Aids:**
- Share screen to show Profile Portal
- Navigate to standalone pages
- Show leaderboard in action
- Display achievement progress
