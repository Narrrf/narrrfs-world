## [Development Wins – 2.5 mins]

"This season was a huge milestone technically.

We:

• Deployed a complete shop system tied to DSPOINC with real-time balance syncing

• Synced rewards across Discord and web automatically — your DSPOINC balance updates instantly whether you play on Discord or the website

• Fixed over 200 bugs with help from our tireless testers and devs — the community has been incredible

• **Completed our Three.js migration** — we're now actively building our 3D game layer with 4 levels fully implemented:
  - **Level 1:** Cheese Temple with 3 riddles, portal mechanics, and physics-based puzzles
  - **Level 2:** The Spawn — Matrix-style white room with weapon galleries, monster displays, and inspection zones
  - **Level 3:** The Hunt — 160x160 arena with monster capture mechanics, progressive scaling, and 10 monsters to catch
  - **Level 4:** The First Shot — First-person shooter with 50-cheese challenge, progressive difficulty, weapon viewmodel, and explosion effects

• **Full trait tracking system** — every level step unlocks traits that sync to your Discord roles and wallet

• **God Mode development tools** — press G to cycle riddle steps, press L to warp between levels instantly

• Integrated legacy Hytopia developers into our 3D pipeline — we're building something special with Three.js + Solana game layer integration.

And that's just phase one. We have detailed technical documentation, a complete riddle system with 6+ riddles across 4 levels, and we're planning Level 5 and beyond.

A huge thank you to our launchpad and dev friends from @Gensuki_, @samuziNFT, and many others here — the journey's been wild, and you helped make it smooth."

## [3D Adventure Game Highlight – 1.5 mins]

"And I want to highlight something I'm really proud of — our 3D adventure game is **live and playable right now** on narrrfs.world.

We've built 4 complete levels, each with:
- **Progressive riddle systems** — find hidden cheese stones, solve puzzles, unlock portals
- **Trait-based progression** — every step unlocks traits that sync across Discord, wallet, and game
- **DSPOINC rewards** — earn up to 2,800 DSPOINC per level completion
- **First-person controls** — smooth movement, shooting mechanics, portal entry
- **Progressive difficulty** — cheeses get smaller and faster, monsters scale up, challenges adapt

Level 4 alone has a 50-cheese shooting challenge where the difficulty ramps up as you progress — cheeses get 40% smaller, 2.5x faster, and much smarter at dodging. It's a real test of skill.

All of this is built with Three.js, fully integrated with our Solana ecosystem, and accessible directly from our website. No downloads, no installations — just connect your wallet and play."
