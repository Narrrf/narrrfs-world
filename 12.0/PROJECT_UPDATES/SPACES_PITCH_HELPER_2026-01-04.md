## 💰 4. The Economy & Staking (1.5 min)

"DSPOINC is our unified currency across all games. Players earn it by:
- Playing games and achieving high scores
- Completing riddles in the 3D game
- Opening chests (both in-game and on profiles)
- Participating in Discord events
- Completing quests and missions

And now — **staking is live!**

Players can freeze their DSPOINC for 3, 6, or 12 months and earn guaranteed rewards. The longer you stake, the higher your reward rate. This creates a deflationary mechanism while rewarding long-term holders.

Staking is fully integrated into the profile system, Discord bot, and admin interface. Players can check their stake status anytime, and rewards are automatically calculated and paid out when stakes mature.

All of this runs on our custom infrastructure — no third-party dependencies, fully transparent, and completely automated."
