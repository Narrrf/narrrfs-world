## 💡 **QUICK TIPS FOR OUTREACH**

1. **Personalize Each Message:**
   - Mention specific things you like about their project
   - Reference shared values or community overlap
   - Show genuine interest, not just transactional

2. **Be Professional But Friendly:**
   - Keep the cheese puns light (we know it's quirky!)
   - Show competence and organization
   - Make it easy for them to say yes

3. **Provide Value:**
   - Explain what's in it for them
   - Highlight mutual benefits
   - Offer cross-promotion opportunities

4. **Follow Up Respectfully:**
   - Wait 3-5 days before following up
   - Keep follow-ups brief and friendly
   - Accept "no" or "not now" gracefully

5. **Make It Easy:**
   - Provide clear asset requirements
   - Offer to draft descriptions if they're busy
   - Handle all the technical work
