# 🛡️ Moderator Onboarding — Mobile

Moderator job: keep Discord safe + route issues fast.

- **DSPOINC / account issues:** collect details → route to Community Manager/staff (Nerd Lab checks)
- **Bugs:** route to `bug-report.html` + require steps + device/browser + screenshot/clip
- **Scams/fake links:** remove + action (warn/timeout/ban) + repost official link
- **Partner issues (`partners.html`):** route to Community Manager + track assets in `12.0/COLLABORATIONS_PARTNERS/PARTNER_ASSET_TRACKER.md`
