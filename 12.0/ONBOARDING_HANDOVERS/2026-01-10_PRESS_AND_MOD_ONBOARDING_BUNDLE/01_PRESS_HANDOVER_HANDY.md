## Handy version (copy/paste)

### What is Narrrfs World?
Narrrfs World is a **web-based gaming ecosystem**: players log in with **Discord**, play games, and earn **DSPOINC** (our universal in-game currency). The ecosystem includes **7 integrated games**, a **Discord bot**, a professional **admin interface (Nerd Lab)**, **store + inventory**, **quests**, **achievements**, **season management**, and a **Partner Portal**.

### Key facts (fast bullets)
- **Games (7):** Tetris, Snake, Space Invaders, Cheese Hunt, Discord Race, Cheese Rumble, 3D Riddle Game
- **Currency:** DSPOINC (earned in gameplay + events; used in store; staking system exists)
- **Discord bot:** community ops + games + economy features
- **Admin / “Nerd Lab”:** operations control panel (users, points, store, quests, seasons, partners)
- **Partner Portal:** `partners.html` is DB-driven; partners are managed via Nerd Lab

### Where to look (public)
- Player hub: `profile.html`
- Partners: `partners.html`
- Admin (requires access): `admin-interface.html`
