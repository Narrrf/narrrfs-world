2025 Foundation

🚀 Community Rebuild, Website Gateway & Mint Path

2025 was the foundation year. Narrrf’s World rebuilt its Discord/community base, prepared the public website, shaped the Genesis Mouse mint path, restored early role and DSPOINC direction, and turned the project from scattered ideas into a connected Web3 gaming ecosystem.

  • Discord structure, roles, moderation, and community systems were rebuilt.
  • Public website became the gateway for mint, roles, games, partners, and information.
  • Genesis Mouse holder lane and VIP/holder access direction became clearer.
  • Early gameplay, events, and partner activity created the first real community loops.
Late 2025 Build Sprint

🧀 3D / Riddle Direction & Gameplay Experiments

The late 2025 sprint expanded the future world direction: riddle gameplay, boss concepts, environment ideas, chest/reward concepts, and action-system planning. Not every experiment became a live feature, but the work helped define the long-term Mouseverse and 3D direction.

  • Phoenix boss behavior and early action-system planning expanded.
  • Chest and reward concepts moved closer to scalable game logic.
  • Environment, climbing, and interaction systems were explored for future gameplay.
  • The future 3D Riddle Game became a major long-term pillar, now targeted later for 2027 alpha direction.
Early 2026 Utility

🧊 DSPOINC Staking System — Historical Launch

DSPOINC staking moved the economy beyond simple rewards. The Stake Lab introduced freezing, reward durations, claim logic, and a clearer reason for players to treat DSPOINC as a longer-term ecosystem resource.

  • Freeze durations from short-term to long-term options.
  • Reward claiming and early-unstake rules introduced.
  • Profile and Stake Lab became part of the economy layer.
  • Staking became one of the permanent systems that should not reset with seasonal leaderboards.
Lab + Identity Layer

🧬 Genesis Lab, Mouse Names & Trait Progression

The Genesis Lab became the deeper holder lane. Verified Genesis Mice moved beyond static ownership: holders could connect identity, custom names, Lab progression, trait upgrades, and future ability direction.

  • Verified Genesis Mouse ownership became the deeper Lab access lane.
  • Custom Mouse names and profile identity became part of holder progression.
  • Trait upgrades and timed research cycles formed the NFT-bound upgrade direction.
  • Genesis progression is treated as permanent and separate from seasonal score resets.
Rewards + Economy

🎁 Reward Chamber & Marketplace Direction

Reward Chamber and marketplace systems helped connect profile actions, lootboxes, DSPOINC, inventory, Lab utility, and admin-controlled reward logic. This created a clearer permanent economy layer around player activity.

  • Marketplace messaging separated Genetic items from protected Genesis Mouse identity.
