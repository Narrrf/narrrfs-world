🧊 DSPOINC Staking Lab

Freeze your DSPOINC for selected durations and earn rewards. The longer you stake, the higher your reward!

How It Works: Freeze your DSPOINC for 1, 3, 6, 12, 24, or 36 months. After the freeze period, receive your original DSPOINC plus a reward bonus. All transactions are tracked in your DSPOINC Journey.

📌 Important Information

✅ No Wallet Connection Required for Staking: DSPOINC staking works directly with your Discord account. Simply log in with Discord to start staking your DSPOINC - no Solana wallet connection needed!

🎴 NFT Collection Feature: The "Your NFT Collection" section below is a separate feature that displays your Narrrf's World NFTs. This feature does not affect staking and is optional. Wallet connection is only needed if you want to view your NFT collection.

💡 Future Development: NFT-based staking features are planned for future updates. For now, DSPOINC staking is independent of NFT ownership.
