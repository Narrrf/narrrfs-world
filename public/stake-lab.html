🔍 How NFT display & verification works
1.
Wallet Connection: Connect your Solana wallet securely to access your NFT collection.
2.
NFT Collection Scan: We use Helius APIs to search your wallet for Narrrfs World: Genesis Genetic and Narrrf Genesis VIP Drop NFTs.
3.
NFT Gallery Display: Use Load My NFTs to view your matching NFTs with images, names, and available traits.
4.
Verification & Secure Save: Use Verify & Save My NFTs to sign a message with your connected Solana wallet, verify your holdership, update your Discord role eligibility, and securely save your verified NFT and Genesis trait data in the Narrrf’s World SQL database for admin review and future ecosystem features.
Ledger users: Open the Solana app on your Ledger, enable Blind Signing, and close Ledger Wallet / Ledger Live before signing.
