Narrrfs World Game Manual

🧀 Cheese Runner Guide

Collect visible cheese, survive the enemy squad, use blue portals wisely, and outplay the CheeseMind AI. Higher levels become smarter, faster, and more tactical — but rewards still follow the normal score system.

🎯

Main Goal

Clear each level by collecting all visible cheese crumbs and Power Cheese. Blue portals are travel lanes only and are not hidden cheese.

🌀

Portals

Use blue portal lanes to escape across the maze. They give no score and are not required for level clear. On higher levels, CheeseMind may punish repeated portal habits.

🧠

CheeseMind AI

Enemies learn only during the current run. They can pressure, predict, cut off routes, and guard key resources. No AI memory is saved after the run.

💰

DSPOINC

Final score converts to DSPOINC with your best active role multiplier. The backend remains authoritative when saving rewards.

🧀 Power Cheese

Enemies flee and become vulnerable. You can earn enemy rewards multiple times, capped at 3 rewarded enemy eats per Power Cheese. Nest enemies cannot be farmed.

🅕 Glyph Boost

Gives +100 score, temporary speed, and brief protection. It does not let you eat enemies.

🍄 Confusion Mushroom

Risk item. Temporarily reverses controls and gives no direct score reward.

🏆 Role Bonus / DSPOINC

🧠 CheeseMind Fairness

CheeseMind is active only during your current run. It watches routes, pressure moments, repeated portal use, and remaining cheese, then changes enemy tactics by level.

Enemy Squad Roles

  • 🔴 Destroyer pressures the mouse.
  • 🟣 Emperor predicts and cuts off routes.
  • 🟢 Invader guards resources and escape lanes.

Privacy / Economy Safe

  • No AI memory is stored after the run.
  • No AI data is written to the database.
  • No extra DSPOINC source is created.

💰 DSPOINC Scores / Rewards

Your raw Cheese Runner score is converted into DSPOINC using your best active role bonus. The backend remains authoritative when saving your final run.

🧮 Reward Formula

floor((score × role multiplier) / 25)

⭐ Score Sources

  • Visible cheese crumb: +10 base score
  • Power Cheese: +50 base score
  • Vulnerable enemy eaten: +200 base score
  • Level clear bonus: +500 base score
  • F Glyph Boost pickup: +100 score
  • Confusion Mushroom: no direct score reward
  • Portals / tunnel lanes: no score

⚡ Power Cheese Cap

  • Power Cheese lets players eat enemies for a short time.
  • Enemy rewards are capped at 3 rewarded enemy eats per Power Cheese.
  • Enemies inside the nest/spawn area cannot be farmed.
