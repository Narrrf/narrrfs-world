6. Genesis Lab & the Two-Lane System

Narrrf’s World is built around two simple lanes. The first lane is open and easy to enter: Discord login, games, events, community battles, rewards, and DSPOINC activity. The second lane begins when a player owns or mints a Genesis Mouse and verifies holder access.

🎮 Lane 1: Discord Player Lane

Players can join with Discord, play seasonal games, enter events, collect DSPOINC, explore Reward Chamber systems, and become part of the community before owning a Genesis Mouse.

🧬 Lane 2: Genesis Holder Lane

Genesis holders can verify their Mouse, name it, enter the Lab, upgrade traits, work with ability progression, and build permanent identity across the ecosystem.

  • 🧬 Verified Genesis Mouse ownership opens the deeper holder lane.
  • 🏷️ Mouse names and identity stay part of long-term player progression.
  • ⚙️ Trait upgrades and ability systems are the foundation for future gameplay utility.
  • ⏱️ Lab upgrades use timed research cycles, claims, and DSPOINC-powered actions.
  • 🎮 Genesis gameplay integrations begin through Discord fights, role systems, ecosystem battles, and progression-linked utility.
  • 🏛️ The larger 3D game direction remains future-facing, with Genesis traits and abilities intended to support deeper game integration as the Q3 2027 alpha direction matures.

This moves Genesis Mice beyond static ownership. A Mouse becomes a player identity, a Lab progression object, and the foundation for ability-based gameplay inside Narrrf’s World.
