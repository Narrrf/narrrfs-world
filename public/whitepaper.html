6. Genesis Lab & the Two-Lane System

Narrrf’s World is built around two simple lanes. The first lane is open and easy to enter: Discord login, ten games, events, community battles, rewards, and DSPOINC activity. The second lane begins when a player owns or mints a Genesis Mouse and verifies holder access.

🎮 Lane 1: Discord Player Lane

Players can join with Discord, play seasonal games, enter MouseFight and community events where eligible, collect DSPOINC, explore Reward Chamber systems, and become part of the community before owning a Genesis Mouse.

🧬 Lane 2: Genesis Holder Lane

Genesis holders can verify their Mouse, name it, enter the Lab, upgrade permanent traits and abilities, manage Genetic Items, prepare MouseFight fighter snapshots, and build permanent identity across the ecosystem.

7B. MouseFight — Genesis Combat as Game #10

MouseFight is the tenth official Narrrf's World game. It uses verified named Genesis mice, permanent Lab progression, and approved Owner Genetic Item selections to build temporary fighter snapshots for Discord PVP challenges and moderator-hosted bracket events.

🏆 Champion Mode

Permanent Genesis progression and approved selected Genetic Items matter through the normal temporary fighter snapshot.

🐭 Underdog Mode

The weaker fighter receives bounded temporary support while real progression remains meaningful.

⚖️ Equalized Mode

Temporary matchup values move toward a shared midpoint without changing permanent traits, abilities, inventory, or ownership.

🎲 Chaos Mode

Both fighters receive randomized fight-only values inside approved ranges. Permanent Genesis and Lab data remain unchanged.

Read-only battle history

The public MouseFight Arena reads stored finished-fight data. It can show season totals, player rankings, Genesis fighter records, battle-mode specialists, participants, bracket paths, round scores, and recorded combat indicators. The website does not run the combat engine or recalculate winners.

Current power and historical success are separate

Strongest Genesis Mice ranks current Lab progression power using permanent traits, permanent abilities, named-mouse and Fitness bonuses, and capped owner Genetic Support. MouseFight rankings measure stored historical battle results such as wins, championships, PVP results, round wins, and fighter usage.

🛡️ Protected MouseFight boundaries

Public presentation does not change Fight Recovery, PVP escrow, settlement, event entry burns, champion rewards, token approval, Genesis ownership, permanent traits or abilities, Lab progression, Genetic Item ownership, authentication, or database structure.
