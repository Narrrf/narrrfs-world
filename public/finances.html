🧀 Economy Layers

How the Season 12 economy is structured

Narrrf's World uses multiple utility layers. Seasonal competition can reset, while permanent systems keep their history and progression.

🎮 Game activity layer

  • Players compete across the Season 12 game ecosystem.
  • Seasonal leaderboards can reset for fair monthly competition.
  • Game score APIs and backend validation decide final score records.
  • Labyrinth Blast uses server-side validation and backend reward logic.

💰 DSPOINC utility layer

  • DSPOINC is used as live in-ecosystem utility balance.
  • Balances, ledgers, staking, and reward history must survive season resets.
  • Frontend pages display utility context; backend remains authoritative.
  • Economy history is permanent project infrastructure, not seasonal score data.

🎁 Rewards layer

  • Reward Chamber, boxes, claims, quests, and events create gameplay loops.
  • Reward history is preserved across season resets.
  • Admins can manage reward systems without touching seasonal score resets.
  • Public copy must never make frontend reward logic authoritative.

🧬 Genesis holder layer

  • Genesis Mice provide identity, access, and holder progression context.
  • Lab upgrades, custom names, trait progress, and inventory are permanent systems.
  • Marketplace items must not be described as mutating Genesis NFTs directly.
  • NFT-bound progression stays controlled through verified Lab upgrade paths.
