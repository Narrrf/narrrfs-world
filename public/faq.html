The basics

💎 What is Narrrf’s World?

⌄

Narrrf’s World is a Web3 gaming ecosystem built around community, games, Discord identity, Genesis Mouse NFTs, DSPOINC rewards, the Genesis Lab, Reward Chamber, staking, partners, and future gameplay direction.

The easiest way to understand it: play first, go deeper later. New users can start with Discord, games, and events. Genesis holders can enter the deeper Lab progression lane.

☀️ What is Season 12?

⌄

Season 12 is the current summer reset direction. It brings fresh leaderboards, a cleaner public page story, the 9-game ecosystem direction, Labyrinth Blast highlight, Genesis Lab, Reward Chamber, DSPOINC economy, staking, and clearer reset messaging.

Season 11 becomes previous/frozen history. Season 12 is the current public context.

🧭 What are the 2 lanes?

⌄

Narrrf’s World has two simple lanes:

  • Lane 1 — Discord Player Lane: log in, play games, join events, earn DSPOINC, open rewards, and build your profile.
  • Lane 2 — Genesis Holder Lane: own/verify a Genesis Mouse, name it, enter the Lab, upgrade traits, and prepare for future ability direction.

🔐 Do I need Discord?

⌄

Yes, Discord is the main player identity layer. It connects profile activity, roles, games, community events, Reward Chamber actions, support, and holder onboarding.

Join here: Narrrf’s World Discord.

🎴 Do I need a Genesis Mouse to play?

⌄

No. You can start as a normal player through Discord, games, rewards, events, and the profile system. A Genesis Mouse unlocks the deeper holder lane: verification, Lab access, custom Mouse names, trait progression, and future ability direction.

🏆 How do leaderboards work?

⌄

Seasonal score games save scores under the active season. The backend reads the active season from tbl_seasons and uses Season 12 fallback where updated.

If a brand-new season has too few scores, some leaderboard views can show a previous frozen leaderboard until enough new-season activity exists.

Open Leaderboard

🔮 Why is Glyph Memory special?

⌄

Glyph Memory is not just another arcade score row. It uses its own memory/time-trial path and needs separate reset/archive handling. That is why we do not blindly treat it like Tetris, Snake, Invaders, Cheese Runner, or Labyrinth Blast.

💣 What are Cheese Race and Cheese Rumble?

⌄

Cheese Race and Cheese Rumble are Discord/community event lanes. They help keep the world active outside normal browser games and are important testing grounds for future role and ability ideas.

⌄

Genesis Lab is the holder progression layer. It loads verified Genesis Mouse identity, displays traits, supports custom Mouse names, tracks trait upgrade state, uses timed research cycles, and prepares the foundation for future ability-based gameplay.

🧬 What are NFT-bound upgrades?

⌄

NFT-bound upgrade direction means your verified Genesis Mouse can become a progression identity inside Narrrf’s World. Names, traits, Lab history, and upgrade state can become part of the holder lane.

Important: marketplace actions should not be described as mutating the Genesis Mouse. NFT-bound progression stays controlled through verified Lab upgrade paths.

DSPOINC + Rewards

Economy, rewards, and permanent progress

💰 What is DSPOINC?

⌄

DSPOINC is the live in-ecosystem utility currency. It connects gameplay rewards, Lab actions, Reward Chamber, staking, marketplace direction, and player activity.

It is not a guaranteed return promise. It is the current ecosystem utility layer.

🎁 What is Reward Chamber?

⌄

Reward Chamber is the profile reward layer. It contains the 3-box reward structure:

  • Box 1: free daily DSPOINC reward loop.
  • Box 2: paid DSPOINC lootbox with weighted reward pools.
  • Box 3: premium claim path that can require admin/mod follow-up.

Open Reward Chamber

🧊 What is DSPOINC staking?

⌄

Staking lets players freeze DSPOINC for a selected period and claim according to the live staking rules. Frozen DSPOINC should not be treated as spendable until it is completed or unstaked.

Current staking is still running on the legacy contract. Season 13 V2 is already visible as a preview in the Stake Lab, but it is not active yet.

The future V2 system introduces day-based lock pools and Genesis holder staking boosts. The same verified Genesis count ladder is now also used for live Discord Genesis tier roles, while staking boosts remain preview-only until Season 13 V2 is officially activated.

Open Stake Lab

🧬 How will Genesis staking boosts work in Season 13 V2?

⌄

Season 13 V2 is planned to turn Genesis mice into staking boosters. One Genesis opens the holder lane, but the first real staking boost starts at two Genesis mice.

0 Genesis: x1.00
1 Genesis: x1.00
2 Genesis: x1.025
3–5 Genesis: x1.05
6–15 Genesis: x1.10
16–29 Genesis: x1.20
30+ Genesis: x1.35

Same-tier rule: to keep the Genesis boost, a V2 staker must stay in the same or higher Genesis tier until claim time. Selling below the starting tier keeps the base reward, but removes the Genesis boost.

This preview is informational until V2 is activated. Final staking rewards and Genesis ownership checks stay backend-authoritative.

Mint Genesis on Gensuki or open the Stake Lab preview.

🧬 What are Genesis Discord tier roles?

⌄

Genesis Discord tier roles are live recognition and utility roles for verified Genesis holders. The Discord bot checks verified Genesis ownership, assigns the highest matching tier role, and removes older Genesis tier roles so every holder keeps one clean tier identity.

The role ladder is based on verified Narrrfs World Genesis mice only. VIP NFTs are not counted for this specific Genesis tier ladder.

Genesis Tier 1: 1 Genesis
Genesis Tier 2: 2 Genesis
Genesis Collector: 3–5 Genesis
Genesis Expert: 6–15 Genesis
Genesis Elite Holder: 16–29 Genesis
Genesis Legend: 30+ Genesis

These Discord roles are live now. The connected Season 13 V2 staking boost system is still a preview until the backend staking contract is officially activated.

Open the Genesis role guide or mint Genesis on Gensuki.

🛒 What can I trade in the marketplace?

⌄

Marketplace direction is for Genetic items and eligible player inventory pieces, not for mutating the Genesis Mouse itself. Boosters are earned through lootboxes/reward systems and should not be described as tradeable unless the live marketplace supports it.

🔄 What resets and what stays safe?

⌄

Seasonal leaderboards can reset so competition feels fresh. Permanent systems stay safe:

  • Genesis Lab progression
  • Mouse custom names
  • NFT-bound trait / upgrade state
  • Reward Chamber history
  • DSPOINC balances
  • staking
  • inventory
  • marketplace state
  • wallet links
  • holder identity
  • profile identity
