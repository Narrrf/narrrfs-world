❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how Narrrf’s World works — from Genesis NFTs and the Lab to games, staking, and progression systems

💎 What is Narrrf’s World?

Narrrf’s World is a Web3 gaming ecosystem where your identity, NFTs, games, and rewards are all connected. Players earn DSPOINC, verify NFTs, upgrade traits in the Genesis Lab, and prepare for future gameplay systems like the 3D Riddle Adventure.

🎴 What do I get when I mint a Genesis NFT?

Genesis NFTs are your core identity and progression units. You unlock:
• access to the Genesis Lab
• 4k HDR version of your NFT with full copyright
• trait upgrades (permanent NFT progression)
• role-based multipliers in games
• access to events, riddles, special giveaways and future gameplay
• eligibility for future 3D game features

🧬 What is the Genesis Lab?

The Genesis Lab is your upgrade system.

You can:
• upgrade traits of your NFTs
• increase permanent NFT power
• claim upgrades after timers finish

Important: upgrades belong to the NFT itself, not the wallet. If the NFT is sold, the upgrade stays with it.

🔐 How does NFT verification work?

You verify your wallet in the Stake Lab. The system scans your NFTs and saves them to your profile.

This supports:
• Phantom, Solflare, Backpack
• Ledger wallets (secure signing)

The Lab always reads from your verified saved NFTs, not live wallet scans.

🎮 What games are available?

Season 11 is live with multiple connected games:
• Tetris

• Discord Race

All games reward DSPOINC and use role-based multipliers.

🌀 What is Glyph?

Glyph is a memory puzzle game that introduces the symbols and logic of the upcoming 3D world. It’s part of the evolving lore and gameplay foundation of Narrrf’s World.

🪙 What is DSPOINC?

DSPOINC is the in-game currency of Narrrf’s World. You earn it by:
• playing games
• completing missions
• participating in events

It is used for:
• Lab instant upgrades
• future gameplay features
• ecosystem rewards

🧊 What is DSPOINC staking?

You can freeze DSPOINC in the Stake Lab for 1–36 months and earn rewards up to 50%.

Rewards are fixed and based on duration.

🏆 What are role multipliers?

Your Discord role boosts your DSPOINC earnings:
• VIP: 2.0x
• Holder: 1.5x
• others scale down

Multipliers apply across all games.

🧀 What is the 3D Riddle Game?

The Cheese Temple is our upcoming 3D adventure game.

Features:
• exploration + riddles
• boss fights
• DSPOINC rewards
• NFT-based gameplay interactions

🧪 Alpha planned for Q3 2026.

📈 Why should I own multiple Genesis NFTs?

More NFTs = more upgradeable traits.

This means:
• more Lab upgrades
• more future gameplay paths
• stronger ecosystem presence

Each NFT becomes its own progression unit.
