🧊 What is DSPOINC Staking?

DSPOINC Staking allows you to freeze your DSPOINC for a selected time period (1, 3, 6, 12, 24, or 36 months) and earn rewards when the freeze period completes. Rewards range from 2% (1 month) up to 50% (36 months). You can view all your stakes, claim rewards, and manage your frozen balance on the Stake Lab page. Your staking overview is also available on your profile page.

❄️ How do I stake my DSPOINC?

Visit the Stake Lab page, log in with Discord, and select the amount you want to freeze and the duration (1-36 months). The system will show you the expected reward based on your selected duration. Once you confirm, your DSPOINC will be frozen and you'll receive the reward when the freeze period completes. Frozen DSPOINC cannot be spent until the freeze period ends or you unstake early (with a 15% penalty).

💰 What are the staking reward rates?

Reward rates increase with longer freeze periods: 1 month: 2%, 3 months: 5%, 6 months: 10%, 12 months: 20%, 24 months: 35%, 36 months: 50%. For example, freezing 100,000 DSPOINC for 36 months will earn you 50,000 DSPOINC in rewards (150,000 total returned). Role multipliers do not apply to staking rewards - the rates are fixed.

🔓 Can I unstake my DSPOINC early?

Yes, you can unstake your DSPOINC early, but there's a 15% penalty on the original amount. You'll receive 85% of your frozen DSPOINC back, but you'll forfeit all rewards. For example, if you freeze 100,000 DSPOINC and unstake early, you'll receive 85,000 DSPOINC back (15,000 penalty). Early unstaking is useful if you need your DSPOINC urgently, but we recommend waiting for the full freeze period to maximize rewards.

🎁 How do I claim my staking rewards?

When your freeze period completes, your stake status changes to "completed" and you can claim your reward on the Stake Lab page. Go to the "Claim Rewards" tab, find your completed stake, and click "Claim Reward". You'll receive your original frozen amount plus the reward bonus. All rewards are automatically added to your DSPOINC balance and appear in your "Recent Score Changes" on your profile page.
