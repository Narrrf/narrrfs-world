📘How the Lab works
Your Lab reads from your saved verified Genesis scan, so your upgrades always start from your real verified mice.
Traits are upgraded one by one, using the exact trait values the selected Genesis mouse really has.
When a timer finishes, the upgrade becomes claimable and your new level is applied when you claim it.
The progression belongs to the NFT path itself, helping preserve long-term Genesis identity.
📈Genesis Timing Guide
Level 1 → 2: 24 hours
Level 2 → 3: 48 hours
Level 3 → 4: 96 hours
Level 4 → 5: 192 hours (8 days)
Level 5 → 16: after 8 days, each next level adds 24 more hours
Level 16+: each next level adds only 1 more hour
Claim rule: finished research must always be claimed manually before the next cycle can begin
Booster rule: Green, Blue, and Red Elixirs reduce active research time by fixed hours
🧬Genetic System Quickstart
Genetic Shop lets players preview every seeded Outfit and Accessories trait and buy missing ones into a Discord-bound inventory.
Genetic Items do not mutate NFTs. They are owned by the verified Discord player profile and progress in a parallel system.
Genetic upgrades use the same progression philosophy as Genesis research, but they run at roughly double speed and currently cap at level 100.
The same Lab booster pool can now accelerate Genetic Items too, so the time-control layer stays unified across both systems.
🧭Two progression lanes
Genesis Lab: NFT-bound, verified holder identity, exact trait progression, long-term on-chain mouse path.
Genetic Inventory: Discord-bound, buyable trait items, faster user progression, future marketplace transfer layer.
Golden rule: the backend remains authoritative for ownership, timers, claim state, instant finish cost, and booster usage.
Safety rule: these two systems stay separated so live Genesis progression is never corrupted by the newer Genetic layer.
