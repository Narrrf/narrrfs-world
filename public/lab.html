📘Start Here: How the Lab works
Your Lab starts with your verified Genesis mice. Use the Holder Verify / Stake Lab flow first, then return here to manage the mice that are saved to your profile.
Select one Genesis mouse to inspect its real traits, current research level, ability progress, custom name, and available milestone reward chests.
Genesis trait upgrades are researched one by one. When a timer finishes, you must manually claim the finished research before starting the next upgrade.
The frontend explains and displays your progress, but the backend remains the authority for ownership, timers, reward eligibility, costs, claims, inventory, and DSPOINC ledger writes.
🧭Two progression lanes
Genesis NFT Lane: Genesis trait levels, Genesis ability levels, custom mouse names, Fitness unlocks, Weapon unlocks, and future Education progression belong to the selected Genesis mouse path.
Discord Lab Inventory Lane: Genetic Items, Store Items, Elixirs, boosters, Reward Chamber drops, and marketplace-owned item state belong to the verified Discord player profile.
Important V1 rule: Genetic Items can support your Lab inventory, but they are not permanently equipped to one specific Genesis mouse yet.
Safety rule: The two lanes stay separated so newer Discord inventory systems cannot corrupt long-term Genesis NFT progression.
📈Genesis unlock map
Trait Lv5+: Fitness lane becomes visible and your mouse starts entering the real long-term progression path.
Trait Lv10: Fitness Journey Chest unlocks with the 500 EMPIRE TOKEN reward.
Trait Lv15: Deep Training Fitness Chest unlocks with the Gun Special Genetic Item, or a DSPOINC fallback if the item cannot be granted again.
Trait Lv20: Final Fitness Journey Chest unlocks with the 500 EMPIRE TOKEN reward, and the Weapon Journey opens.
Trait Lv30 direction: Education is the future high-level knowledge lane and should stay treated as future expansion unless the backend/UI is explicitly activated.
🎁Fitness Journey reward chests
Lv10 Fitness Chest: 500 EMPIRE TOKEN.
Lv15 Fitness Chest: Gun Special Genetic Item.
Lv15 fallback: 1,000,000 DSPOINC if the Gun Special Genetic Item is already owned or cannot be granted again.
Lv20 Fitness Chest: 500 EMPIRE TOKEN and the transition into the Weapon Journey.
Claim rule: Fitness Journey chests are one-time milestone rewards and the backend checks your verified Genesis ownership, required trait level, and previous claim history.
⚔️Weapon Journey reward chests
Unlock rule: Weapon rewards require your selected Genesis mouse path to reach highest Genesis trait level Lv20 first.
Weapon lanes: ATK, DEF, and SPECIAL each have their own ability level progress and milestone reward checks.
Lv1 Weapon chests: ATK Lv1, DEF Lv1, and SPECIAL Lv1 currently reward 25,000 DSPOINC each.
Higher milestones: Weapon Lv5, Lv10, Lv25, and Lv50 milestone tiers exist, but they stay locked until the matching ability reaches the required level.
Backend rule: A visible chest does not mean the frontend can grant it. The claim endpoint verifies ability category, ability level, ownership, duplicate claim state, and DSPOINC delivery.
⏱️Research timing and speed-ups
Level 1 → 2: 24 hours.
Level 2 → 3: 48 hours.
Level 3 → 4: 96 hours.
Level 4 → 5: 192 hours / 8 days.
Level 5 → 16: after 8 days, each next level adds 24 more hours.
Level 16+: each next level adds only 1 more hour.
Instant Finish: spends DSPOINC to finish an active timer immediately. The backend always recalculates the final price.
Elixirs: Green, Blue, and Red Elixirs reduce active research time by fixed hours and consume 1 inventory item.
Lootbox-only utility: Elixirs are not sold directly in the store. They come from Reward Chamber lootboxes and special Lab reward drops.
🧬Genetic Items and Marketplace rules
Genetic Shop lets Discord-logged-in players buy missing Outfit and Accessories trait items into their Discord-bound Lab inventory.
Genetic Items do not mutate on-chain Genesis NFTs. They progress in the parallel Discord inventory lane.
Genetic Item upgrades are timed research. Normal start upgrades are free timed research, while Instant Finish is the paid DSPOINC shortcut.
Marketplace listings transfer the full owned item state between players while respecting the 2-copy exact-trait ownership limit.
Listed Genetic Items cannot be upgraded, claimed, boosted, or instant-finished until they are no longer listed.
❓Common Lab questions
Why can’t I see my Genesis mouse?
Verify or refresh your Genesis scan first, then return to My Genesis Lab. The Lab only works from verified saved ownership data.
Why can’t I start another Genesis upgrade?
Genesis progression allows one active research cycle per mouse path. Claim finished research first, then start the next cycle.
Why can’t I claim a Fitness chest?
Your selected Genesis mouse has not reached the required trait level yet, or that one-time milestone chest was already claimed.
Why can’t I claim a Weapon chest?
Weapon chests require Trait Lv20 first, then the matching ATK, DEF, or SPECIAL ability level for that milestone.
Why did I get DSPOINC instead of an item?
Some rewards use a fallback when an item is already owned or cannot be granted again. The backend decides the safe delivery path.
What is NFT-bound vs Discord-bound?
Genesis traits and abilities belong to the Genesis mouse path. Genetic Items and reward inventory belong to the Discord profile lane.
