When Do You Need Wallet Login?

✅ Discord Login is Enough For:

  • Playing all website games (Tetris, Snake, Space Invaders, etc.)
  • Earning DSPOINC and competing on leaderboards
  • Accessing your profile and stats
  • Using the store and inventory system

🔗 Wallet Login is Required For:

  • Holder Verification: Verify your NFT ownership and get Discord roles (🏆 Holder or 🎴 VIP Holder)
  • 3D Riddle Game Access: Enter the coming 3D Riddle Cheese Temple game
  • Cheese Hunt Mode: Play the 3D Riddle Game in reward mode with your NFT traits
🧬

What This Verification Does

1. Discord Role Granting: Automatically grants you the correct Discord role based on your NFT collection:

  • Genesis Collection → 🏆 Holder role
  • VIP Collection → 🎴 VIP Holder role

2. Trait Snapshot for 3D Riddle Game: Scans and saves your NFT traits (attributes, properties, metadata) to your account. These traits will be used when you play the 3D Riddle Cheese Temple game in Cheese Hunt mode (reward mode with trait-based bonuses).

💡 Note: Your traits are securely stored and will enhance your gameplay experience in the upcoming 3D Riddle Game. Regular website games work perfectly fine with just Discord login!
