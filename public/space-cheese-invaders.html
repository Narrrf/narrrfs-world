Space Invaders features 9 progressive waves with special enemies!

👾 Waves 1-3 (Normal)
Regular invaders
Formation patterns, increasing difficulty
Standard rewards
🔥 Wave 4: Phoenix Wave
Special Phoenix enemies!
Lay eggs → Mini-Phoenix spawn
Bonus rewards!
🧀 Wave 8: GIANT CHEESE BOSS
Multi-layer cheese boss!
3 HP layers, shoots back!
HUGE rewards!
Wave progression gets harder! Phoenix every 4 waves, Giant Boss every 8!

🔥 Special Enemies

🔥 Phoenix Invaders
Spawn on Wave 4, 12, 16, etc. Lay eggs that hatch into Mini-Phoenix. Kill Phoenix before eggs hatch!
🐣 Phoenix Eggs
Phoenix lay 2 eggs. After ~5 seconds, eggs hatch into Mini-Phoenix (faster, harder!).
🐤 Mini-Phoenix
Hatched from eggs. Faster and more aggressive than regular Phoenix!
🧀 Giant Cheese Boss
Wave 8, 16, 24... Multi-layer boss with 3 HP. Shoots back! Defeat all layers to win!

💪 Power-Ups & Weapons

❤️ Hearts
Drop from Giant Cheese Boss. Collect for bonus health or power-ups!
🔫 Laser Beam
Press '2' key. Pierces through multiple enemies! Limited ammo (5 shots).
💣 Screen Bomb
Press '3' key. Clears ALL enemies on screen! Limited ammo (3 bombs).
